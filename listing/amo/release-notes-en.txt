New: Zapazka Lite now checks the text you type, not only the field. A card number, an IBAN, a private key, an API key or token, or "password: ..." in the text is not kept, and digits kept before a card number was complete are removed. The toolbar popup lists every text box on the page as kept or refused, with the reason; earlier refusals of the site are listed too (field name and reason, never the text). The toolbar badge shows how many text boxes are protected on the page. Bulgarian field names such as парола and ПИН are never kept.
