Zapazka Lite keeps what you type in text boxes, so a crashed browser, a closed tab or an expired session cannot take a long reply with it. Come back to the empty box and press Restore, or put the text back from the toolbar button.

NEW IN 1.1: IT CHECKS WHAT YOU TYPED, AND SHOWS WHAT IT REFUSES
Other form-recovery add-ons skip a field only by its type or name. Zapazka Lite also reads the text: a card number (Luhn check), an IBAN, a private key, an API key or token, or "password: ..." typed into an ordinary message box is not kept. If the first digits of a card number were kept at a pause before the number was complete, they are removed again.

And you can see it, instead of taking it on trust:
- The toolbar popup lists every text box on the page as "kept as you type" or "refused", with the reason (for example "the text holds a valid card number").
- Earlier refusals on the site are listed too: the field name and the reason, never the text.
- The toolbar badge shows how many text boxes are protected on the page, or "off" on an excluded site, so you know it is watching before you need it.

WHAT IT KEEPS
- Text areas (the big reply and comment boxes), as you type, in this Firefox profile only, for 7 days.
- Never password, card, security-code or one-time-code fields, fields named for tokens, PINs or secrets (also in Bulgarian: парола, ПИН, номер на карта, код за потвърждение), hidden, disabled or read-only fields, or anything in private windows.
- Two sign-in sites are excluded from the start.

HOW IT WORKS
- Type as usual. About half a second after you stop, the text is saved locally.
- Come back to the empty box: a small chip offers "Restore 228 characters from 2 h ago?". One click puts the text back and the page sees it as typed.
- The toolbar popup lists the texts kept on the current site with Put back, Copy and Delete.

PRIVATE
No server, no account, no analytics, no network access. Texts are stored in Firefox's local extension storage, not encrypted, like Firefox's own form history. Uninstalling the add-on deletes everything it kept. Data collection declared to Mozilla: none.

LITE AND FULL
Zapazka Lite is complete for text boxes. The paid edition, Zapazka (https://pnscripts.com/marketplace/firefox-zapazka), also keeps rich editors and one-line fields, up to 50 versions per field, a searchable page of all kept texts, your own never-keep words and a refusal ledger across all sites.

Needs Firefox 142 or newer on desktop. English and Bulgarian, following Firefox's language. Open source (MIT): https://github.com/pnscripts/firefox-zapazka-lite. Made by PN Scripts, with AI assistance; checked with 19 automated tests, Mozilla's addons-linter and a run in a real Firefox.
